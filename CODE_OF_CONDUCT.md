# Code of Conduct

We pledge to provide a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and report security issues privately.

Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory language, sexualized
conduct, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone represents the
NamoID community. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation to enforce these standards fairly.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@namoid.in`. Reports will be handled
promptly and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
